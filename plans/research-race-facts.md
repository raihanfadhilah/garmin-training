# Ultra X Madeira 25K — verified race facts

Source of truth: the [official 2026 Race Information Pack (PDF)](https://ultra-x.co/uploads/2025/12/Ultra-X-Maderia-NB-2026-Race-Information-Pack-V1.pdf),
read directly. Where the marketing page and the race pack disagree, the race pack wins.

## CORRECTION — the cut-off is 11.5 hours, not 6h15

The event page's "generous time allowances of 2.5 hours per 10 km" implies 6h15 for 25 km.
**That derivation is wrong.** The race pack's own cut-off table is explicit:

| Race | Cut-off |
|---|---|
| 110 km | 36 hours |
| 60 km | 18 hours |
| 50 km | 14.5 hours |
| **25 km** | **11.5 hours** |

The pack's own wording is "2.5hrs **to 4 hrs** per 10km" — the 25K is set at the generous
end (4.6 h per 10 km). **The cut-off is not a constraint.** An estimated 5-6.5 h finish has
roughly 5 hours of margin.

**Implication: the plan should be built around finishing well and not getting injured, not
around beating a clock.** This meaningfully de-risks the whole project, and it means there
is no reason to race the early descent for time.

## Mandatory kit — it exists, and it is enforced

**Correcting a second error:** Ultra X's UK *Spring Trail Series* PDF says "no mandatory kit
list for the 12km & 25km distances." **That does not apply to Madeira.** The Madeira race
pack is unambiguous:

- **Rule 13:** "Runners must carry with them AT ALL TIMES the mandatory equipment required."
- **Rule 14:** "There will be mandatory kit checks at the start, on route, and at the finish.
  Failure to comply with the kit checks will result in disqualification."
- Kit checks are also spot-checked at registration and at random during the race.
- The running pack "must be large enough to carry the mandatory items on the kit list."

**The itemised Madeira kit list is not in this pack — it is a separate document.** Get it.
Pack weight sets the load for the weighted downhill-walk protocol and needs training in.

## Other rules that affect strategy

- **No pacers** (rule 1), **no crew** (rule 17), **no drop bags along the trail** (rule 6).
  He is self-sufficient between the ~2 checkpoints.
- **Must check in at every checkpoint** or DQ (rule 7).
- Race holdall max 10 kg, transported start → finish.
- Race briefing issued by PDF in the week before; short briefing at the start line.

## Terrain (from race-pack photography)

Images in the official pack show: **stone staircases with fixed rope handrails**, exposed
volcanic rock ridgelines, and root-heavy laurel forest singletrack. This corroborates the
4/5 technicality rating and matters for two reasons:

1. **Stone steps feature heavily** — which raises the value of stair descending as training
   (its specificity here is much better than its general reputation).
2. **Exposed ridge sections at 9 degC in November rain** are a genuine hypothermia risk on a
   5-6 h day for a 56 kg athlete. Shell and warm layer are safety items regardless of what
   the kit list mandates.

## The 11:00 start changes three things

Sunset is **18:17**, civil twilight ends **18:41**.

| Finish | Clock | Light |
|---|---|---|
| 6:00 | 17:00 | daylight |
| 6:30 | 17:30 | daylight |
| **7:00** | **18:00** | **dusk** |
| 7:30 | 18:30 | **dark** |

1. **Headtorch is a real safety item, not a formality** — a 7h15+ finish is in the dark on
   exposed cliff paths. Carry it regardless of what the kit list says.
2. **Pre-race fuelling inverts** — a full unhurried morning instead of the usual 5 a.m.
   scramble. Big advantage; see `research-pacing.md`.
3. **The whole race sits in the warmest part of the day**, and Machico (finish) runs warmer
   than the north-coast start.

## Mandatory kit — no Madeira-specific list exists

Exhaustive search of the organiser's site and CDN found **no Madeira kit list PDF**. The
verified Ultra X European template (Finland 2026, which has a "25" column) requires: running
pack; **1.5 L hydration**; shoes; **durable waterproof jacket with hood, sealed seams**; watch;
emergency blanket; **800 kcal emergency food reserve**; charged phone with GPX; **personal
beaker >=15 cl — bottles and flasks explicitly NOT accepted**; spare warm layer. Extreme-weather
additions communicated 1 week out.
